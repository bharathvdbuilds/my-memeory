About

More Than Martial Arts. A Foundation for Life.

Unity Martial Arts Club in Ananthnagar, Bengaluru trains children, teenagers, and adults. Classes build fitness, discipline, confidence, concentration, and practical skills.

  • Structured karate, kata, and kumite
  • Self-defence for boys and girls
  • Tournament and championship preparation
  • Belt examination preparation
  • Training for different age groups

Programs

Train with purpose.

Karate, kata, kumite, and self-defence for kids, teenagers, and adults.

01

Karate Training

Technique, coordination, fitness, and discipline, built through traditional fundamentals. Classes give students a clear structure to practise and improve.

02

Kata Training

Improve movement, balance, precision, memory, and traditional forms. Students repeat each form until the sequence is controlled and remembered.

03

Kumite Training

Sparring fundamentals: timing, distance, and control, with preparation for students who want to compete. Practice stays measured and parent-appropriate.

04

Self-Defence

Practical awareness and defence skills for kids, teens, and adults, boys and girls. Sessions focus on reading a situation and using simple, effective responses.
