About

A joyful studio, since 2009.

Dance & Joy Studio opened in 2009. For more than 15 years it has been a joyful, safe space in Chandapura where children and adults build confidence, fitness and stage presence.

From a first class to the annual show, batches stay friendly and encouraging. Ages start at 3, and there is no upper limit — if you want to dance, you belong here.
